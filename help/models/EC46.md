# EC46

*ECMWF EC46 extended range (weekly ensemble mean; data by Open-Meteo.com)*

## Why it matters

The European Centre's extended range forecast: its ensemble run 46 days ahead, which is beyond what a weather forecast can say about a given day but not about a week. Each chart is the mean of the 51 members for a week (Monday to Sunday), as a departure from the ECMWF's own climate or as the values, which shows the pattern the ensemble leans to: a warm week over the east, a wet one over the plains. The data comes from Open-Meteo's seasonal service (CC BY 4.0, based on ECMWF data) at a 4 degree grid over North America.
