# RAP-ANL

*NOAA/NCEP RUC / RAP hourly analysis (NCEI archive), 13 km*

## Why it matters

The hourly analysis of the Rapid Update Cycle (to April 2012) and the Rapid Refresh that followed it, 13 km over North America, from 5 January 2005 on. An analysis for every hour, finer in time than the 6 hourly global ones, which is useful for following a storm or a front through a day. Only the analysis is here, not a forecast.
