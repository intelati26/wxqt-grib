# MF-SST

*Meteo-France sea surface temperature 1/12 degree (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The sea surface temperature of Meteo-France's global ocean system, on a 1/12 degree grid (about 9 km): the eddies of the Gulf Stream, the warm pool a hurricane feeds on, the upwelling of the Pacific coast. One run a day; the first day of each run is the analysis, then the forecast to 10 days, every 6 hours. It is a forecast of the water, so a chart a few days ahead shows where the Gulf Stream and the loop current will be.
