# RRFS

*NOAA/NCEP RRFS 3 km*

## Why it matters

NOAA's new 3 kilometre forecast system for the continental US, built to replace the Rapid Refresh, the HRRR and several others. At 3 km it can draw individual lines of storms and their rotation, which the global models cannot, and it updates every hour.
