# ARPEGE

*Meteo-France ARPEGE global 0.25 degree (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

Meteo-France's global model, run four times a day on a grid that is stretched to be finest over Europe (here a regular 0.25 degree one). It is hourly to 48 hours, then 3 hourly to 4 days. A European view of the world: useful for the Atlantic and the Mediterranean, less for the rest, and short beside the GFS and the Euro.
