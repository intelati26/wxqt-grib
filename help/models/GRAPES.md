# GRAPES

*CMA GRAPES global 0.125 degree (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The China Meteorological Administration's global model, here on a 0.125 degree grid, 3 hourly to 5 days, four times a day. It is the least used of the global models outside China, which is the reason to look: a pattern it shares with the others is firmer, one only it shows is worth a second look. Its charts here include the wind gusts, CAPE and cloud cover.
