# MF-CURRENT

*Meteo-France ocean currents and sea level 1/12 degree (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The surface currents and the height of the sea of Meteo-France's global ocean system, on a 1/12 degree grid (about 9 km), hourly to 10 days. The current chart shows speed in colour with streamlines for the flow: the Gulf Stream, the Loop Current in the Gulf of Mexico, the eddies that break off them. The sea surface height shows the tide, the eddies (warm ones stand higher than cold ones) and the lift of the sea; the third chart is the part of the height the air pressure makes alone (the inverse barometer: a low pressure of 10 mb lifts the sea by about 10 cm, which is how a storm surge begins).
