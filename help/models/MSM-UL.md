# MSM-UL

*JMA MSM 5 km Japan, pressure levels (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The upper-air half of the Japan Meteorological Agency's meso-scale model (the surface half is the model "MSM"): the height, temperature, humidity and wind of 16 pressure levels from 1000 to 100 mb at 5 km over Japan and the seas round it. A run every 3 hours, 3 hourly to 39 hours. Use it for the jet over Japan, the troughs ahead of a typhoon's recurvature and the 850 mb front of the baiu rains.
