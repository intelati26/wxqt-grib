# RRFS-AK

*NOAA/NCEP RRFS Alaska, 3 km*

## Why it matters

NOAA's Rapid Refresh Forecast System for Alaska: a 3 km model that runs every hour and forecasts out to 84 hours from the 00, 06, 12 and 18Z runs. It shares its open data bucket and its runs with the one for the contiguous United States. It is the successor to the Rapid Refresh and HRRR, run on a newer core, and it is the one to read for storms and fronts a day or two ahead where its grid is fine enough to show them.
