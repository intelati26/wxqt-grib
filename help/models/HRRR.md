# HRRR

*NOAA/NCEP HRRR 3 km*

## Why it matters

NOAA's high resolution model for the contiguous United States: 3 km, run every hour, forecasting out to 48 hours on the runs that go farthest. It carries radar reflectivity, the rotation in storms and the instability that thunderstorms feed on, so it is the model to read on a severe weather day for what storms will do over the next several hours. It is a short-range model: beyond a day or so the GFS and Euro are better guides.
