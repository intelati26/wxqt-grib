# GSM

*JMA GSM global 0.5 degree (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The Japan Meteorological Agency's global model, here on a coarse 0.5 degree grid, every 6 hours, with its 00Z and 12Z runs out 11 days. Its strength is the western Pacific: typhoons, the East Asian jet and the troughs that later reach North America show up in it early. It is coarse, so read the pattern, not the detail, and it has no gusts or CAPE here.
