# CFS

*NOAA/NCEP CFSv2 seasonal forecast, member 1, 1 degree*

## Why it matters

NOAA's Climate Forecast System, version 2, a nine month forecast made four times a day, here on a 1 degree grid every 6 hours (the first member). One run says little about a given day weeks ahead, but the map shows the lean of the pattern: where the ridge sits, which way the storm track runs. It is the raw model; the CFS30 model here is its next month as departures from normal.
