# HGEFS

*NOAA/NCEP Hybrid GEFS ensemble mean, 0.25 degree (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

NOAA's Hybrid GEFS: the average of the GEFS (the physical ensemble) and the AIGEFS (the machine-learning one) taken together, a mean over both kinds of members. Averaging two different kinds of model smooths the errors that each makes alone, so it is the first chart to read for the pattern 3 to 10 days out. It is a mean, so it is smoother than any single outcome; the GEFS and Euro ensemble charts show the spread between members. Here it is on a 0.25 degree grid every 6 hours.
