# RRFSD

*SPC severe weather fields from the RRFS (RRFSD)*

## Why it matters

The mesoanalysis fields, computed from RRFS forecasts instead of observations: the same instability and shear charts, for the next three days. It answers the question the analysis cannot: where the ingredients are expected to come together, and when.
