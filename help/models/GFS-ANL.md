# GFS-ANL

*NOAA/NCEP GFS analysis (NCEI archive), 0.5 degree*

## Why it matters

The GFS analysis of the years before NOAA's open data bucket: 2007 to mid 2014, four a day, 0.5 degree. An analysis is the model's best fit to the observations at that hour, so it is the closest thing to a map of what the atmosphere was doing. Some analyses are missing from the archive (27 April 2011 at 00Z, for example), and that run shows an error.
