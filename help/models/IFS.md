# IFS

*ECMWF IFS 0.1 degree (0.25 degree for runs before 20 Oct 2026; open data, CC BY 4.0)*

## Why it matters

The European Centre's global model, the benchmark the others are measured against for forecasts of three to ten days. When it and the GFS disagree on a pattern it is worth knowing why. The open data here is the 0.1 degree run (the 0.25 degree one before 20 October 2026), released under a Creative Commons licence.
