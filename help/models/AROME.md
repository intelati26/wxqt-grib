# AROME

*Meteo-France AROME France 2.5 km (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

Meteo-France's high resolution model over France and the countries round it: 2.5 km, a new run every 3 hours, hourly out to 51 hours. At that grid it draws thunderstorms, sea breezes, the winds funneled through the Rhone valley and the fog of the valleys, which the coarse models smooth away. A short-range model: read it for the next two days, and the global models beyond.
