# SPCREFS

*SPC REFS ensemble products (open Zarr data)*

## Why it matters

The Storm Prediction Center's own view of the REFS: means, maxima, chances, and paintballs that draw each member's storms in its own colour so you can see where they agree. These are the products its forecasters use for the next two days of convection.
