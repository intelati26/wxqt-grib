# SPCGEFS

*SPC post-processing of the GEFS: daily calibrated thunder, machine-learning severe*

## Why it matters

The same corrected probabilities from the GEFS: a daily thunderstorm chance out to eight days, and machine-learning estimates of severe weather. They reach further ahead than the storm-scale guidance, which is where an outlook for days four to eight begins.
