# NAM-ANL

*NOAA/NCEP NAM analysis (NCEI archive), 12 km*

## Why it matters

The analysis of the NAM, NOAA's 12 km regional model for North America, from 4 March 2004 to April 2020, four a day. It is finer than the global analyses and so shows the fronts and the jets of a past event more sharply. Each run's file also has the 3 and 6 hour forecasts.
