# GFS

*NOAA/NCEP GFS 0.25 degree*

## Why it matters

NOAA's global model, run four times a day out to 16 days at 0.25 degrees. It is the free backbone of US forecasting: many other products start from it, so a chart here is the first place a pattern shows up days ahead. It runs on a coarse grid, so it will not resolve individual storms.
