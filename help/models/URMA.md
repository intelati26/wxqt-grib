# URMA

*NOAA/NCEP URMA surface analysis, 2.5 km*

## Why it matters

The UnRestricted Mesoscale Analysis: the RTMA made again a little later, with observations that were not yet in when the RTMA ran. It is the more complete picture of the surface weather of an hour (2 m temperature and dew point, 10 m wind and gust, visibility, cloud cover), 2.5 km over the contiguous United States, and the one to use for a past day.
