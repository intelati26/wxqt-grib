# AIFS

*ECMWF AIFS 0.25 degree (an AI model; open data, CC BY 4.0)*

## Why it matters

ECMWF's machine-learning forecast model. It matches the physical model closely at a fraction of the computing, and its mistakes differ from the physical model's, which makes the pair useful together. Treat a fine detail with caution: it is smoother than a physical model.
