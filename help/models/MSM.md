# MSM

*JMA MSM 5 km Japan (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The Japan Meteorological Agency's meso-scale model: 5 km over Japan and the seas round it, a run every 3 hours, hourly to 39 hours (to 78 at 00 and 12Z). It is the finer of JMA's two models and is the one to read for typhoons near Japan, the rain of the baiu front and the winds of the Japan Alps. Surface fields only here.
