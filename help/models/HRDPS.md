# HRDPS

*ECCC HRDPS 2.5 km (Canada)*

## Why it matters

Canada's high resolution model: 2.5 km over Canada and the northern United States, run four times a day out to 48 hours. At that grid it draws storms, lake effects and the winds of the mountains that the coarse global models smooth away, and the northern border states and the Great Lakes are well inside it. It is a short-range model, so use it for the next two days and look to the GFS or the Euro beyond.
