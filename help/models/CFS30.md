# CFS30

*NOAA/NCEP CFSv2 the next days against its normal (a week to 90 days; the mean of the four newest runs)*

## Why it matters

What NOAA's seasonal model expects of the next week, two weeks, month, two months or three months (the forecast hour is the stretch), against its own normal for these days: the temperature as colors (warm or cool) and the precipitation as lines (green wetter, brown drier, in millimeters a day). A month ahead the weather of a day is out of reach; what is left is the lean of the pattern, which a stretch of cold or dry weather is often part of. It is the mean of the member 1 of the four newest runs (each a forecast of nine months, six hours apart), so the lean is a guess, not a promise; the Plume window shows how those runs differ from each other.
