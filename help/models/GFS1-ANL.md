# GFS1-ANL

*NOAA/NCEP GFS analysis (NCEI archive), 1 degree*

## Why it matters

The GFS analysis at 1 degree, from 4 March 2004 to 15 May 2020, four a day, with the 3 and 6 hour forecasts of each run. It reaches back farther than the 0.5 degree analysis, which is finer where it has the years (2007 to mid 2014). Use it to look at a past day, such as a tornado outbreak, with the standard charts.
