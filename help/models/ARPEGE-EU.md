# ARPEGE-EU

*Meteo-France ARPEGE Europe 0.1 degree (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

Meteo-France's global model on the part of its stretched grid that is finest, over Europe and the North Atlantic (about 0.1 degree), a run every 6 hours and hourly out to 102 hours. It is finer than the global ARPEGE and runs further than AROME, so it is the one to read for the pattern over Europe three or four days ahead. Surface fields only here.
