# GEOS-FP

*NASA GMAO GEOS-FP 0.25 degree*

## Why it matters

NASA's own global forecast system, run by a different centre with a different model from the ones NOAA, ECMWF and Canada run. It is built around the atmosphere's composition as much as its weather: its output carries dust, smoke, sea salt and ozone fields as well, which the weather models do not. Here it has charts of surface dust, dust in the column, dust and total aerosol optical thickness, fine particles (PM2.5), smoke, sulfate and sea salt, in 3 hour averages: the Saharan plume crossing the Atlantic, Asian dust, and the smoke of large fires are what it is for. Its weather charts are a fourth, independent opinion on the large-scale pattern.
