# REFS

*NOAA/NCEP REFS, 5 member 3 km ensemble*

## Why it matters

Five runs of the RRFS from varied starting points: a storm-scale ensemble. A single 3 km run puts a storm in one place; the members show how much that placement can be trusted, and the ready-made products give the chance of strong storms, heavy rain or snow nearby. It is the successor to the HREF.
