# NBM

*NOAA/NWS National Blend of Models v4, 2.5 km*

## Why it matters

The National Blend of Models is a statistically corrected blend of many models, built to be the starting point for the National Weather Service's own forecasts. Of the models here it is the nearest to what the forecast offices actually issue, and it gives the likelihood of an outcome, not just a single answer.
