# HRRR-AK

*NOAA/NCEP HRRR Alaska 3 km*

## Why it matters

The 3 km High-Resolution Rapid Refresh for Alaska: a run every 3 hours, forecasting out to 48 hours. The same family as the HRRR for the lower 48, and the finest model over the state, so it is the one for storms and winds near the coast and the passes. The far western Aleutians, west of the date line, are cut from the charts here.
