# GDPS

*CMC GDPS global 15 km (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

Environment and Climate Change Canada's global model: 15 km, run twice a day (00 and 12Z) out to 10 days. It is the Canadian counterpart of the GFS and the Euro and a useful third voice on the large patterns, and it is the better of the two for Canada's own weather, from the Arctic air to the lows that come in off the Pacific. Every third hour is shown here (the pressure levels are not kept hourly).
