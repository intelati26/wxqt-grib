# RRFS-NA

*NOAA/NCEP RRFS North America, 13 km*

## Why it matters

NOAA's Rapid Refresh Forecast System for North America: a 13 km model that runs every hour and forecasts out to 84 hours from the 00, 06, 12 and 18Z runs. It covers the whole continent on a coarser grid than the regional ones, and only the 00, 06, 12 and 18Z runs have it. It is the successor to the Rapid Refresh and HRRR, run on a newer core, and it is the one to read for storms and fronts a day or two ahead where its grid is fine enough to show them.
