# GFS-WAVE

*NOAA/NCEP GFS-Wave 0.16 degree*

## Why it matters

The sea state the GFS winds produce: significant wave height, period and direction. It matters for marine forecasts, surf, and anywhere swell from a distant storm arrives days before the weather does.
