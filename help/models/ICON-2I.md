# ICON-2I

*ARPAE ICON-2I 2.2 km Italy (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The ICON model run by Italy's weather services (ARPAE and Italia Meteo) at 2.2 km over Italy and the seas round it, twice a day (00 and 12Z) out to 72 hours. It resolves the thunderstorms of the Alps, the Po valley fog and the Mediterranean lows that bring heavy rain, which the larger ICON models cannot show in detail. Six pressure levels, from 1000 to 250 hPa.
