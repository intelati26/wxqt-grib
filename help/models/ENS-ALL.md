# ENS-ALL

*All ensembles pooled: ECMWF ENS, GEFS and GEPS (102 members), 0.5 degree*

## Why it matters

Three ensembles run by three centres (ECMWF's 50 members, NOAA's GEFS with 31 and Canada's GEPS with 21), pooled as one set of 102 outcomes for the same valid time. Their models and their starting points differ, so the pool spreads wider where the centres disagree than any one of them does, and agreement among all three is the strongest sign that a pattern is trusted. It is the idea behind NAEFS, the North American Ensemble Forecast System. The spaghetti charts color each centre's members in a family of their own, so a split between the centres is visible at a glance.
