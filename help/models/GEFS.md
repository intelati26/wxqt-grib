# GEFS

*NOAA/NCEP GEFS 30 member ensemble, 0.5 degree*

## Why it matters

The GFS run many times from slightly different starting points. How far the runs spread apart is the uncertainty: tight means the pattern is trusted, wide means it is not. The member picker shows any single run, and the chance charts count how many members reach a threshold, which is how forecasts become probabilities.
