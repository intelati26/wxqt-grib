# ERA5

*ECMWF ERA5 reanalysis (NCAR archive), 0.25 degree*

## Why it matters

The European Centre's reanalysis: the weather of every hour from 1940 on, remade with a modern model and every observation it could find, on a 0.25 degree grid. It is the standard record of what the atmosphere did. The copy here is NCAR's, which ends in March 2023, and a field is read from files of a gigabyte or more a piece, so the first one is slow.
