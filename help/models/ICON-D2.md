# ICON-D2

*DWD ICON-D2 2.2 km (Germany and central Europe)*

## Why it matters

The German Weather Service's finest model: 2.2 km over Germany and the countries round it, a new run every 3 hours, hourly out to 48 hours. It resolves thunderstorms directly instead of guessing at them, which is why it is the one to read for a line of storms over central Europe. Only the surface fields are here (temperature, dew point, wind, gusts, pressure, CAPE and cloud); its upper air is on a grid this program does not read.
