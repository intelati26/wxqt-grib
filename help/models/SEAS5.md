# SEAS5

*ECMWF SEAS5 seasonal forecast (monthly ensemble mean; data by Open-Meteo.com)*

## Why it matters

The European Centre's seasonal forecast: a 51 member ensemble run seven months ahead each month. A single day is out of reach, but the months ahead can lean warm or cool, wet or dry, mostly through the oceans (El Nino, the Atlantic and Pacific temperatures). Each chart is the mean of the members for a calendar month, as a departure from the ECMWF's own climate or as the values. The data comes from Open-Meteo's seasonal service (CC BY 4.0, based on ECMWF data) at a 4 degree grid over North America.
