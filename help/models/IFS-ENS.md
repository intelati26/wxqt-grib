# IFS-ENS

*ECMWF IFS ensemble (ENS), 50 members, 0.25 degree (open data, CC BY 4.0)*

## Why it matters

The European Centre's physical model run 51 times from slightly different starting points (here its 50 perturbed members). How far the members spread is the uncertainty of the forecast, and a chart with every member's line on it shows the outcomes that are still possible. Where the Euro and the GFS ensemble (GEFS) both spread widely the pattern is truly uncertain; where they agree and are tight it is trusted.
