# NARR

*NOAA/NCEP North American Regional Reanalysis (NCEI archive), 32 km*

## Why it matters

The North American Regional Reanalysis: a record of the continent's weather every 3 hours from January 1979 to September 2014, made by running one model over the whole period with the observations fed in. At 32 km it is finer than the global reanalyses of its time. It is the one for looking at a historical storm with consistent charts, though it stops in 2014.
