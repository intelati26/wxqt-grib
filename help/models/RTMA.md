# RTMA

*NOAA/NCEP RTMA surface analysis, 2.5 km*

## Why it matters

The Real-Time Mesoscale Analysis: NOAA's hourly map of the surface weather, 2.5 km over the contiguous United States, made by fitting a model to the stations, radar and satellite reports of that hour. It is not a forecast but the best picture of what the 2 m temperature, dew point, 10 m wind and gust, visibility and cloud cover were. Use it to see where a front or a dry line actually is.
