# SFCOA

*SPC mesoanalysis (SFCOA-HRRR)*

## Why it matters

The SPC mesoanalysis: an hourly picture of the storm environment (instability, shear, helicity, the tornado and hail parameters) made by correcting a model background with the latest surface observations. It is what forecasters look at to decide whether storms that form will be organised, so it is the first stop on a severe weather day.
