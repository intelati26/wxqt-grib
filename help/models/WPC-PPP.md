# WPC-PPP

*WPC probabilistic precipitation portal*

## Why it matters

The Weather Prediction Center's probabilistic precipitation portal: how much rain, snow or ice may fall in 24 to 72 hours, as the expected amount, a percentile or the chance of an amount. NOAA says it comes from the National Blend of Models, adjusted to the official forecast.
