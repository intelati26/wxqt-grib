# GEPS

*ECCC GEPS 21 member ensemble, 0.5 degree*

## Why it matters

Canada's global ensemble, 21 members, made by a different centre with a different model. When it and the GEFS agree the confidence is higher than either alone; where they disagree is where the forecast is least certain. Environment Canada serves it openly.
