# AROME-HD

*Meteo-France AROME France HD 1.3 km (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The same Meteo-France AROME at its finest, 1.3 km over France, a new run every 3 hours, hourly to 51 hours. It is surface only here (2 m temperature and humidity, 10 m wind and gusts, CAPE), but the extra detail shows in valleys, coasts and cities where the 2.5 km model is already smoothing. Use it for a local look, not for the large-scale pattern.
