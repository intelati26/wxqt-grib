# UKMO

*UK Met Office global 10 km (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

The Met Office's global model, the Unified Model, at about 10 km: one of the finest of the global models on the free list. It is run four times a day, hourly for the first 54 hours, and its 00Z and 12Z runs go 7 days. A good third opinion where the GFS and the Euro differ, especially on the lows that come in across the North Atlantic.
