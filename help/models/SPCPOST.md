# SPCPOST

*SPC post-processing of the REFS: calibrated thunder and severe guidance*

## Why it matters

Raw model output says how much rain or what wind; forecasters need the chance of a tornado, damaging wind, hail or thunder near a point. The Storm Prediction Center turns the REFS into those probabilities and corrects them against past events, which is the guidance behind its own outlooks.
