# ICON-EU

*DWD ICON-EU 6.5 km (Europe)*

## Why it matters

The German Weather Service's regional model for Europe: 6.5 km, a new run every 3 hours, hourly out to 78 hours. It is finer than the global ICON and has the upper air that the very fine ICON-D2 lacks here, so it is the one to read for the pattern over Europe, the Atlantic coast and the Mediterranean in the next three days.
