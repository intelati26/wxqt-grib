# HAFSB

*NOAA/NCEP HAFS-B, 2 km storm-following grid*

## Why it matters

The second version of NOAA's hurricane model, run beside HAFS-A with different settings. Where the two agree on track or intensity the guidance is more trustworthy; where they differ shows how much the answer depends on the model's choices.
