# CFS-ANL

*NOAA/NCEP CFS analysis (CDAS), 1 degree*

## Why it matters

The analysis of NOAA's Climate Forecast System: the model's best estimate of the atmosphere four times a day, from 22 April 2023 on, about a day behind. It is a way to look at what the weather was, with the same charts as a forecast, on a 1 degree grid. For older dates use the other analyses and reanalyses under Archive.
