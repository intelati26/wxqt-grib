# MF-WAVE

*Meteo-France MFWAM global waves 1/12 degree (via Open-Meteo)*

## Why it matters

Meteo-France's global wave model (MFWAM) on a 1/12 degree grid (about 9 km), the finest global waves in the program: the height of all the waves, of the wind sea (waves the local wind is making), of the main swell and of the second swell (waves that left a distant storm days ago), each with its period and the direction it travels. Three hourly to 10 days, a run at 00 and 12Z. The swell charts show where a long swell from a storm far away will arrive; the wind-sea chart shows the chop of the wind where you are.
