# Home screen order, layout and the toolbar

## Home screen (Settings > Home Screen Order)

The home screen is arranged in **zones** of a layout. At the top of the tab, **pick a layout**, then drag the sections into its
zones (or click a section for a menu: "Move ... to zone ..."). Three lists give the order inside the columns:

- **Image column:** drag to reorder, tick to show (the MRMS radar picture is one of these).
- **Forecast column**, below the conditions and hazards: drag to reorder, tick to show. The seven day forecast always shows. The
  hourly graph and the forecast point are two of its rows.
- **Text column:** drag to reorder, tick to show.

The **MRMS thumbnail area** is either around your location or all of the contiguous United States.

Changes show on the main screen when Settings is closed.

## Toolbar (Settings > Toolbar Order)

The **Toolbar style** list has three styles: icons only, icons with names (grouped), or a menu bar. In the grouped and menu
styles the tools are sorted into **groups**: drag a group to move it, double-click it to rename it. In the *Icons only* style,
tick "Show the selected group as one dropdown button" to turn the selected group into one button that opens a menu of its
entries: fewer icons on the home screen.

If a tool you need seems to be missing, it is probably in a group that is collapsed into a dropdown: check this tab first.
