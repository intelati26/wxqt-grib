# Checks, presets, favorites and saved views

Four ways to get back to a chart you like. All of them are kept in the settings of this computer.

## The Checks picker ("Checks...")

Builds a chart by ticking: the chart itself (a model's charts are grouped into kinds, with the level, the hour, the type or the
limit as lists beside them), then the **lines**, **wind barbs** and **fills** to put on top of it (heights, pressure, thickness,
winds of other levels, a second fill such as dewpoint or humidity drawn translucent over the chart's own). A line under the
choices says what is ticked.

- Beside each **fill** is a color strip (opens the color scale editor: see *Colors*) and a "from" value: the fill is clear
  under that value and tinted from it up, so it does not hide the chart under it.
- Beside each **line** is a style button: color, weight, dashes, the interval, and whether numbers are written on it.
- The **search box** finds a chart by any word of its name.
- The **star** puts a chart in your favorites (and takes it out). Favorites have a group of their own at the top of the chart
  picker.

## Presets

A preset is a chart plus what you ticked on it, under a name. **Save checks as preset...** asks for a name and keeps the
chart and its lines and barbs; **Starters** is a short list the program provides to start from; your own are listed with them,
and each has a **Delete**. A preset made on one model can be put on another: the program finds that model's own chart for the
same checks where it has one.

## Saved views (the Model Viewer's "Saved views")

A saved view is the model, the chart, the area, and the lines and barbs ticked on it. **Save this view...** asks for a name (a
name already used is replaced); each saved view has **Show** and **Delete** under its name. Showing a view on another model
switches to that model first. A view does not keep the time on show or a variant such as a change or a maximum.

## Which to use

Favorites for a chart you open often; a preset for a chart with the extras you always want on it; a saved view for the whole
screen as you left it (model, area and extras).
