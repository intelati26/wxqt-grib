# Saved locations

Settings > **Locations** keeps the places the program works around.

## Adding and editing

- **Enter City** looks a place up by name (**Show on map** and **Show the place on the map** check where it landed). A place is
  a name, a latitude (north +) and a longitude (west -).
- **Edit location** changes a place's name or point. The latitude must be between -90 and 90 and the longitude between -180
  and 180. **Move the point and leave the radar site and the forecast office as they are:** they are looked up again for the
  new point.
- Each place has a **radar site** (the map centre) and a **forecast office (WFO)**; they are found from the point, and you can
  change them.

## What uses them

The home screen's conditions and forecast, the radar centre and the forecast point page use your saved places. A place's
coordinates go to the services that answer for a point: the National Weather Service (`api.weather.gov`) for its forecast and
forecast office, and Open-Meteo for some graphs.
