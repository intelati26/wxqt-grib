# Natural Earth

`resourceCreation/res/atlantic.bin` (the coastlines and country borders on the Atlantic hurricane map) is made from the Natural Earth
1:50m coastline and land boundary lines, clipped to the Atlantic basin and simplified by `resourceCreation/createAtlanticCoast.py`.

Natural Earth data is in the public domain ("Made with Natural Earth", naturalearthdata.com). No permission or attribution is required;
it is credited here as a courtesy.
