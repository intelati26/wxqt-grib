# Natural Earth

`resourceCreation/res/nhc_basins.bin` (the coastlines and country borders on the hurricane map: Atlantic and Eastern / Central Pacific) is made from the Natural Earth
1:50m coastline and land boundary lines, clipped to those basins and simplified by `resourceCreation/createBasinCoast.py`.

Natural Earth data is in the public domain ("Made with Natural Earth", naturalearthdata.com). No permission or attribution is required;
it is credited here as a courtesy.
