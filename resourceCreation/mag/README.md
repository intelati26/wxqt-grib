# The model guidance site (MAG) maps, as the reference for the recreated charts

MAG is likely to go away, so what was read from it is kept here: `mag_inventory.json` (23 models, 566 products, each with its label), `captions.json` (the layer list printed on the bottom of each image, read by OCR, so a word can be a little off: "HGHT" is sometimes "HBHT", "EMSL" "MMSL").

The scripts (they expect the files in /tmp/claude-1000/mag and the images in docs/mag-reference; edit the paths): `scrape.py` the inventory, `grab2.py` one image of every product (waits between requests), `composition.py` the OCR of the captions, `mkdoc.py` -> docs/mag-composition.md, `recreate.py` -> docs/maps-to-recreate.md (the list: what is recreated, what is to do), `parity.py` the layers each recreated chart has against the layers its MAG caption names, `compare.py` the MAG image and ours side by side from the same run and hour (`DEMO_RUN=YYYYMMDDHH tests/gfs/demo.sh`).

## What the side-by-side comparisons found (2026-10-09)
Real differences, fixed: the precipitation maps lacked the sea level pressure and 1000-500 mb thickness lines; the relative humidity charts lacked wind barbs; the 200 / 250 / 300 mb charts had barbs where MAG has the isotachs and heights only; the precipitation type chart was an amount where MAG's is a rate (with the thickness lines); the 1 km radar was one color ramp where MAG colors the echoes by what is falling (rain greens to purple, snow blues, sleet purples, freezing rain reds); two RRFS charts had lines MAG's do not (925 mb temperature and wind, the plain precipitation rate); the ensemble mean precipitation had lines where MAG's has the spread.
Not a match yet: all 30 GEFS mean / spread maps are mean AND spread together; ours are the mean alone, so they are not counted as recreated.
Style, not content, and so far left alone: MAG shades in coarse bands (relative humidity only above 70 %, temperature in 10 degree steps) where ours is a smooth ramp; MAG draws the barbs close together; the CONUS maps are Lambert conformal with a lat / lon graticule and brown borders, ours Mercator; the legend is at the left, vertical. A "MAG look" option (banded ramps at MAG's steps) would close most of that.
